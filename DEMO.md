# 10-minute walkthrough

0:00 Open the preview on a phone. Hero: the tagline, tonight's rate, Book direct. Point out the badge.
1:00 "What do you need?" Tap **A week in summer** → the Book page opens with Sat-to-Sat dates and the live quote: nights, weekly 10%, launch 10%, cleaning fee, total before tax.
2:30 Change the dates to a July 4 weekend of 2 nights: the quote says four nights minimum. Change to 4: $325 nights.
3:30 Back. Tap **The home** tab: the shot list (every grey plate is a photo you will take), features, straight talk, rules.
5:00 **Rates**: season strip, every month and event, snowbird months. "No login, no surprise fees."
6:00 Tap **Ask**: "Is it available?" → routes to a human; "Where exactly is it?" → answer + area link.
7:00 **Area**: six real spots, FAQ.
8:00 **More → Launch audit**: today vs. new flow with Try-it links; the decisions list.
9:00 Airplane mode. Reload: rates and the home still open; an unknown page shows the branded offline screen.
9:30 Share → Add to Home Screen. Open from the icon: full screen, tab bar, no browser chrome.
